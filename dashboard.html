How Key Shelf works

Add a game using common title words. Search URLs are generated for all five stores, or you can paste your own filtered searches. New games start with one edition. Enable Track multiple editions in Manage to select additional editions and optional separate search queries. Identical queries run once per store, and selected editions are matched using their titles.

First-page-only checks are enabled by default. Turn that setting off to read all search pages up to your configured limit. Eneba seller scans are enabled by default; available Gamivo seller pages are checked to distinguish regular and SMART prices. Unavailable products are recorded from the search without opening their seller pages. Every matching listing remains in the shelf, including ignored regions and out-of-stock offers.

Matching uses whole words in product titles. Unrelated search results are discarded before saving or seller visits. Bundle titles and combined game names such as “Priest Simulator x Crime Simulator” use the Bundles edition. Accounts are included only when you select Accounts or Keys and Accounts in Manage. Each selected type has its own prices and alerts.

The first successful reading establishes a baseline. Later scans record price drops, newly discovered offers below the previous lowest price, and loss of the cheapest available offer. Unchanged results and increases refresh the shelf quietly.

Explicit “out of stock” is used immediately. A disappearing seller or listing needs two complete successful scans before it is treated as unavailable. Partial scans, page limits, layout errors and verification failures retain previous data. A stale badge means the current availability is not verified.

Gameseal uses Seal Pass by default; enable “Gameseal: use normal prices” for its regular price. “Show lowest price regardless of region” includes every region, including Unknown, while retaining the region label. Gamivo uses regular prices unless you enable SMART. Confirmed SMART prices are highlighted red. Expand a store and use Price history on each listing to see its recorded price and stock changes. Changing the price basis establishes a fresh baseline to avoid false sale alerts.

Search prices can exclude service fees, payment fees or taxes. Known fees can be entered in settings for estimated totals. Always confirm the region and final price at checkout. No purchases or cart actions are performed.

Data stays in Firefox local storage and your exported backups. Scheduled checks require Firefox to be open. Permanent installation requires the signed XPI from Mozilla. See the included private-signing guide. Export a backup before removing a temporary installation.
