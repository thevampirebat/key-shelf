Key Shelf privacy
Games, templates, settings, price histories and stock records are stored in Firefox local storage and user-exported JSON backups. Your search terms and requested URLs are sent to the chosen retailer websites to obtain results and seller prices. Retailers may process ordinary page requests, cookies and sessions under their own policies. Key Shelf has no analytics, tracking server, remote executable code or cloud backup service. The Ko-fi support page is opened only on a user click. Delete games through Manage or remove the extension to remove local extension data; separately delete any exported backups if desired.
